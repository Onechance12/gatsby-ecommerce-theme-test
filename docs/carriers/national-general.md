## Filing notes

- Use the homeowners claims line for ordinary residential policies.
- If the policy is identified as a master policy and includes control or loan references, use 800-824-8562. A National General representative matched Vega's policy to Lender Services and provided this number on 2026-07-10.
- Treat restricted-number callbacks as unrecognized caller ID and confirm the carrier before selecting a pending callback case.
- Do not treat a callback request as a filed claim. A claim/reference number is still required.
